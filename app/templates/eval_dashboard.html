What Our Evaluations Found & What We Changed

Direct findings from running multi-turn personas against real MCP endpoints and our concrete architectural adaptations:

1. Shared Phones & Family Conflicts
Finding: In Sonari, elderly citizens often use grandsons' or CSC operators' phones. If an active application already exists on that number, blind submission failed with database conflict errors.
Fix: Implemented proactive active application detection in check_application_status and safe withdrawal via withdraw_application.
2. Underage Tea-Stall Inquiries
Finding: Citizens like Haren Bora (57 yrs) frequently inquired based on informal rumors that pension begins at 55.
Fix: Created standalone check_eligibility tool that calculates exact age against Indian Standard Time and explains statutory 60-year criteria respectfully without polluting the database.
3. Small-Screen Bank Typo Recovery
Finding: Small screens and keypad issues caused common IFSC / bank account length errors.
Fix: Added instant regex formatting validation with helpful feedback (e.g. SBIN0003077 11-character format) enabling agents to self-correct in a single turn.
4. Officer Rationale & Statutory SLA
Finding: Discretionary rejections without recorded reasons left applicants confused, and backlog queues breached the 15-day RTPS statutory window.
Fix: Enforced mandatory justification note in decide_application, sorted officer queues by oldest submission first, and exposed real-time SLA countdowns.
