Application Process at a Glance

Our portal is specifically designed for elderly citizens, shared family phones, and rural CSC kiosks.

1
Mobile & OTP
Enter your 10-digit mobile number and verify via a 6-digit SMS OTP. Grandson or family phones welcome.
2
Personal & Bank
Provide applicant name, date of birth, village, active bank account (9–18 digits) and 11-digit IFSC code.
3
Age Proof Upload
Attach a clear photo or PDF copy of Aadhaar Card, Voter Photo ID, School Certificate, or Gaonburah Certificate.
4
Instant Acknowledgment
Receive your official Application Number. Under the RTPS Act, the BDO must review your case within 15 days.
