About the Purvanchal Old Age Pension Scheme

State Social Assistance Programme · Statutory Welfare Gazette Notification 2023/SW/402

The Old Age Pension Scheme is a flagship social security initiative implemented by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Purvanchal. For thousands of elderly citizens across our state, particularly widows, rural agricultural workers, and disadvantaged families, this scheme provides a critical lifeline ensuring basic nutritional and medical dignity in their senior years.

Monthly Financial Assistance

₹250 per month credited directly into the senior citizen's active individual savings bank account through the Public Financial Management System (PFMS) / Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

15-Day Statutory SLA Guarantee

Under the Purvanchal Right to Public Services (RTPS) Act, the Block Development Officer must review every case within 15 calendar days. Any application unreviewed after 15 days automatically stands DEEMED_APPROVED by state law.

1. Eligibility Criteria

  • Age Threshold: The applicant must be 60 years of age or older on the date of application submission. Age is calculated against Indian Standard Time (IST).
  • Domicile: Must be a permanent resident of Purvanchal residing in one of the approved administrative blocks.
  • Bank Account: Applicant must hold an individual savings bank account in any commercial or regional rural bank with an active 11-digit IFSC code.
  • Income & Social Status: Designed for seniors without regular government or formal pension subsistence.

3. Accepted Age Proof Documentation

Any one of the following official documents is accepted as proof of age during submission (JPG, PNG, or PDF format up to 5 MB):

  • Aadhaar Card (UIDAI) showing full Date of Birth or Year of Birth.
  • Electoral Photo Identity Card (Voter ID / EPIC) issued by the Election Commission of India.
  • School Leaving Certificate or High School Examination Board Certificate.
  • Official Age & Identity Certificate issued by the recognized Village Head (Gaonburah / Gaon Panchayat President).

4. Fair-Use & Shared Phone Policy

We recognize that many elderly citizens do not own personal smartphones and rely on their children, grandchildren, or village Common Service Center (CSC) kiosk operators. The portal permits phone sharing for OTP verification; however, each 10-digit mobile number may hold at most one active application (Pending, Approved, or Deemed Approved) at a time. If an applicant needs to rectify an error, a pending application may be withdrawn from the status portal to allow immediate re-submission.
